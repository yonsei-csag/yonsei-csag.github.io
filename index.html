The Computer Systems & Architecture Group at Yonsei University (csag@yonsei), affiliated with the School of Computing at Yonsei University, focuses on various research topics spanning computer systems, security, and architecture. csag@yonsei have been formed to pursue top-tier, interdisciplinary computer systems, security, and architecture research by bringing together faculty members and graduate students having high expertise in compilers, operating systems, systems security, and computer architecture.

We are always seeking prospective PhD and MSc students interested in computer systems, security, and architecture research. In case you are interested in joining our group, please send your CV and research interests to FIXME@yonsei.ac.kr.
